The Three-Pillar Memorization Routine

Designed to prevent memory decay and ensure solid, permanent recall.

1. Daily Sabaq

Reading new verses to the teacher with zero Tajweed errors before memorization begins, ensuring accuracy.

2. Daily Sabqi

Reciting recent memorization from the current Juz every single session to reinforce short-term memory.

3. Cumulative Manzil

Systematic scheduled recitation of older Juz to maintain permanent recall across the entire Quran.
