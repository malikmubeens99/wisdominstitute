Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our classes.

How are the classes conducted?
Classes take place 1-on-1 via Zoom or Google Meet with real-time digital Quran screen sharing and teacher feedback.
Can absolute beginners join?
Yes. We start complete beginners and young children with the Noorani Qaida syllabus, focusing on letter recognition and phonetics.
Are female teachers available?
Yes. Qualified female teachers are available for female students and young children.
What is the duration of each class?
Standard classes are 30 to 45 minutes long, scheduled 2, 3, or 5 days a week based on your preference.
