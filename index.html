You register with your basic details without providing any credit card or payment information. After the third class, you decide whether to enroll in a regular plan.

All classes at Wisdom Institute are strictly private 1-on-1 live sessions conducted via Zoom. Your teacher's entire attention is on one learner for the full 30-minute session.

Our teachers are based in Pakistan, hold formal Tajweed certifications, and possess approximately 3 to 7 years of teaching experience. We offer both male and female teachers.
