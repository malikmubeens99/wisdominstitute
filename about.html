About Wisdom Institute

An online Quran education initiative by the Wisdom Hope Foundation.

Our Mission

"A world where no one is unable to read the Quran."

With 2 years of dedicated educational service, Wisdom Institute connects international Muslim families worldwide with qualified, Tajweed-certified teachers from Pakistan for private 1-on-1 Zoom classes.

We currently serve over 100+ students across 15+ countries, including the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Europe.
